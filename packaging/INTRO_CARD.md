## EdgeAI Model Hub

[![Models](https://img.shields.io/badge/models-16%2B-c41230)](https://huggingface.co/TexasInstruments-EdgeAI) [![Hardware](https://img.shields.io/badge/hardware-TI%20MPU-c41230)](https://huggingface.co/TexasInstruments-EdgeAI) [![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-mixed%20OSS-c41230)](https://huggingface.co/TexasInstruments-EdgeAI)

Pre-trained, **hardware-optimized** AI models for TI edge devices — compiled and benchmarked for production inference, with no cloud dependency.

- Hardware-optimized for TI MPU processors
- Benchmarked on real TI hardware
- Portable, vendor-neutral model format
- Automated scripts for model preparation & deployment

**Use cases:** Automotive · Aerospace & Defense · Industrial · Surveillance · Robotics · Edge IoT

**16+ model families** spanning image classification (ResNet, ViT, ConvNeXt, DINO/DINOv2, MobileNetV3) and object detection (YOLO11/26/v8/X, RT-DETRv2, RTMDet, RF-DETR, DETR & more) — validated on TI's AM62A, AM67A, TDA4VM, AM68A, and AM69A/TDA4VH device families.

Models ship under a mix of open-source licenses (Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause, AGPL-3.0) — see each model's page for the exact terms.
